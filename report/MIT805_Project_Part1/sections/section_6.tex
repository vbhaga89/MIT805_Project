\section{Business/Societal Value and Insights}

The dataset enables the development and testing of AML detection systems at a realistic scale. Insights derived from the data may improve suspicious transaction monitoring, reduce fraud related costs, improve risk management, support regulatory compliance and improve operational efficiency by prioritising high-risk transactions for investigations. Improved AML systems can assist in combating financial crimes such as fraud, terrorist financing, human trafficking and organised crime, thereby protecting the integrity of financial systems and reducing the economic impact of illicit financial activities.

Limitations: 

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The dataset is synthetic and may not fully represent real-world financial behaviour or every laundering technique, affecting models trained on the data in real situations.
    \item The dataset’s large size requires substantial storage, and processing resources, particularly when analysing the full dataset.
\end{enumerate}
